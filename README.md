# Dusk Till Dawn

You're on your way back home, the road is blocked so you take a "shortcut". On your way back home, your car runs out of fuel and you're stuck on a mountain until you're able to contact help, that is at dawn, because of the cell service.

But wait, you're not alone, something else is with you, can you outlive it?


## Story: 

- Base: A guy was heading back from work using a shortcut, his car lost fuel so now he is looking for a nearby gas station and some help in general, he finds out the gas station that was supposed to assist him is an abandoned one. Now he is looking for any kind of assistance, he finds a radio tower but he has to wait until dawn to be able to contact the authorities. Now he remains in survival mode, hiding from the monster lurking in the woods and trying his hardest not to fall victim to it.

- The monster: Alex Blake is a normal guy who was leading a normal life, goes to work on time, gets his work done. But he's also talking to a woman online, he is ABSOLUTELY inlove with the woman. One day, he bought a flight ticket to go see her, until he was met with a terrible plane accident, he survived miraculously, but he lost his sight. He stayed stranded there waiting for help, but he couldnt survive until dawn, he ate anything he found just to try and survive but he couldn't so a mysterious spirit turned him into a monster so he could live longer, but with the twist of staying on the mountain forever.

- The player: Jeremy Downs, a psychologist also living a normal life, was on his way back home to his wife and kids, traffic was hell tho. Jeremy couldn't stay patient so he decided to take a shortcut given to him by the maps app on his phone, he ran low on gas so he stopped in the middle of nowhere to try getting to a gas station. Unfortunately for him, things are not the way they seemed.

## Controls:

- W: forward
- A: left
- S: back
- D: right
- R: crouch
- Shift: sprint
- Space: jump
- F: toggle flashlight 
- E: interact
